FAQ

Before we
start.

A few simple answers before you send a project brief. If your project doesn't fit these, that's completely fine.

How does a project usually start?

Send a short description of what you want to build. We can figure out the scope, approach and starting estimate from there — you don't need a technical specification.

Can you work with an existing website or app?

Yes. Existing projects, redesigns, fixes, integrations and improvements can all be discussed alongside new builds.

How long does a website or bot take?

It depends on the scope and integrations. Smaller builds can move quickly, while larger systems need more planning and testing. You'll get a clearer estimate after the requirements are understood.

Do you provide hosting and deployment?

Yes, deployment can be included where appropriate. Hosting and third-party service costs are separate when they apply.

Can I request something not listed in Services?

Absolutely. The service list is a starting point, not a limit. Tell me what you're trying to achieve and we can work out the right solution.
